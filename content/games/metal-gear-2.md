# Metal Gear 2: Solid Snake

![Metal Gear 2 cover art](/images/mgs/metal-gear-2.png)

<span class="playtime">~10 hours</span>

**Released:** July 20, 1990 (MSX2)

**Platform:** MSX2

Set years after the original, Solid Snake is called back into action to infiltrate **Zanzibar Land**, where scientist **Dr. Kio Marv** has been kidnapped. Snake must rescue him and uncover the region's secret — a confrontation that brings him face to face with his former commander, **Big Boss**. The game refines the stealth formula with a radar system, crawling, and more intricate level design.
