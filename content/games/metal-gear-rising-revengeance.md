# Metal Gear Rising: Revengeance

![Metal Gear Rising Revengeance cover art](/images/mgs/metal-gear-rising-revengeance.png)

<span class="playtime">8 hours</span>

**Released:** February 19, 2013 (PlayStation 3, 360, PC)

**Platform:** PlayStation 3, 360, PC

A spin-off developed by PlatinumGames that shifts the series from stealth to action. Set after the events of Metal Gear Solid 4, the game follows cyborg **Raiden**, who works for a private military company and becomes embroiled in a conflict with the mercenary group **Desperado Enforcement**. Combat revolves around high-speed swordplay and the **Blade Mode**, which allows precise cutting of enemies and objects.
