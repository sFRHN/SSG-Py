# Metal Gear Solid 2: Sons of Liberty

![Metal Gear Solid 2 cover art](/images/mgs/metal-gear-solid-2.png)

<span class="playtime">20 hours</span>

**Released:** November 13, 2001 (PlayStation 2)

**Platform:** PlayStation 2, PC

The story begins aboard a tanker, where Solid Snake investigates a new Metal Gear, and then shifts to the offshore cleanup facility **Big Shell**, seized by terrorists demanding the body of a dead president. Rookie agent **Raiden** is dispatched to infiltrate the facility. The game's narrative revolves around memes, information control, and the manipulation of reality.
