# Metal Gear Solid 3: Snake Eater

![Metal Gear Solid 3 cover art](/images/mgs/metal-gear-solid-3.png)

<span class="playtime">23.6 hours</span>

**Released:** November 17, 2004 (PlayStation 2)

**Platform:** PlayStation 2, PlayStation 3, 360, PC

A prequel set in 1964 during the Cold War. CIA operative **Naked Snake** is airdropped into the Soviet jungle of Tselinoyarsk on Operation Snake Eater, a mission to rescue a scientist and eliminate his former mentor, **The Boss**. The game emphasizes survival — managing camouflage, stamina, and injuries — as Snake hunts his target through the wilderness.
