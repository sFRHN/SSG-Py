# Metal Gear Solid 4: Guns of the Patriots

![Metal Gear Solid 4 cover art](/images/mgs/metal-gear-solid-4.png)

<span class="playtime">25 hours</span>

**Released:** June 12, 2008 (PlayStation 3)

**Platform:** PlayStation 3

Set in 2014, the world is controlled by private military companies fighting endless proxy wars. Solid Snake, now an aging veteran, is drawn back into battle to stop **Liquid Ocelot**, who plans to seize control of the **Patriots** system. The game follows Snake across war-torn locations as he confronts the legacy of his past.
