# Metal Gear Solid: Peace Walker

![Metal Gear Solid Peace Walker cover art](/images/mgs/metal-gear-solid-peace-walker.png)

<span class="playtime">30 hours</span>

**Released:** April 29, 2010 (PlayStation Portable)

**Platform:** PlayStation Portable, PlayStation 3, 360

Continuing Big Boss's story in 1974, the game takes place in **Costa Rica**, where he is hired to investigate a mysterious military force operating in the country. The mission leads to the founding of **Militaires Sans Frontières** and the construction of **Mother Base**. Gameplay focuses on short, repeatable missions, base development, and confronting the AI weapon **Peace Walker**.
