# Metal Gear Solid: Portable Ops

![Metal Gear Solid Portable Ops cover art](/images/mgs/metal-gear-solid-portable-ops.png)

<span class="playtime">15 hours</span>

**Released:** December 5, 2006 (PlayStation Portable)

**Platform:** PlayStation Portable

Set in 1970, between the events of Snake Eater and Peace Walker. **Big Boss** is captured during a mission and must escape from a military prison in South America. To survive, he recruits soldiers into a growing squad. The game centers on squad-based stealth and the origins of Big Boss's private army.
