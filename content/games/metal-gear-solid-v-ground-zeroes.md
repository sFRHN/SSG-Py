# Metal Gear Solid V: Ground Zeroes

![Metal Gear Solid V Ground Zeroes cover art](/images/mgs/metal-gear-solid-v-ground-zeroes.png)

<span class="playtime">5.5 hours</span>

**Released:** March 18, 2014 (PlayStation 3/4, 360, Xbox One, PC)

**Platform:** PlayStation 3/4, 360, Xbox One, PC

The prologue to _The Phantom Pain_, set in 1975. **Big Boss** infiltrates **Camp Omega**, a U.S. naval base in Cuba, to rescue **Paz** and **Chico**, who are being held by the organization **Cipher**. The game is an open-ended mission that introduces the series' systemic stealth gameplay and serves as a direct lead-in to _The Phantom Pain_.
