# Metal Gear Solid V: The Phantom Pain

![Metal Gear Solid V The Phantom Pain cover art](/images/mgs/metal-gear-solid-v-the-phantom-pain.png)

<span class="playtime">134.8 hours</span>

**Released:** September 1, 2015 (PlayStation 3/4, 360, Xbox One, PC)

**Platform:** PlayStation 3/4, 360, Xbox One, PC

Set in 1984, **Big Boss** wakes from a nine-year coma and sets out to build a new military force, establishing **Mother Base** and launching operations across open-world environments in **Afghanistan** and **Central Africa**. The game emphasizes infiltration, extraction, and base management as Big Boss pursues the man responsible for the destruction of his former unit.
