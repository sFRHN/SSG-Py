# Metal Gear Solid

![Metal Gear Solid cover art](/images/mgs/metal-gear-solid.png)

<span class="playtime">17 hours</span>

**Released:** September 3, 1998 (PlayStation)

**Platform:** PlayStation, PC

Solid Snake returns from retirement to infiltrate the Alaskan island facility of **Shadow Moses**, where the rogue special forces unit **FOXHOUND** has taken hostages and threatens to launch a nuclear strike with a prototype Metal Gear. Snake must disable the weapon and stop the unit, confronting its members — including **Psycho Mantis**, **Sniper Wolf**, and the unit's leader, **Liquid Snake**.
