Solid Snake, a rookie operative of FOXHOUND, is sent to the fortress nation of Outer Heaven to investigate the development of a nuclear-capable walking battle tank and to rescue the captured agent Gray Fox. Rather than engaging enemies head-on, the player must avoid detection by sneaking past guards, hiding in lockers, and using the environment to stay out of sight. The game's stealth-focused design laid the groundwork for the entire genre.
